HOW THE DETECTION ALGORITHM WORKS  (plain-language overview)
BASC construction-start detector, ~/Personal/THE AMAZIN ALOGRITHM/basc
Written 2026-09-10 from the code and logs as they stand today.
For the long, section-by-section version see MODEL_STRUCTURE.txt (2026-09-07).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. THE JOB
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
Given a data-centre site and a fortnight, say whether heavy construction
(clearing, grading, foundations) has started there, using only what could
have been seen from Sentinel-2 imagery up to that fortnight.

It is NOT an alarm. It ranks sites into a human review queue. The number
that matters is "how many real starts land in the queue", not accuracy.

Time is cut into half-months called periods: 2024-03-H1 is 1-15 March,
2024-03-H2 is 16-31 March. 24 periods per year.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. THE PIPELINE, STEP BY STEP
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
Step 1  Decide WHERE to look                       (aoi.py, inject_zones.py)
  One resolver picks the patch of ground per site, in priority order:
    site plan  -> union of the PLANNED building footprints, if a georeferenced
                  plan existed before the observation date
    parcel     -> the validated Regrid parcel
    point box  -> a square around the best coordinate
  It always returns three zones from one fetch:
    building     the planned footprints (or a placebo footprint on negatives)
    development  the whole parcel
    context      a ring of land AROUND the parcel where nobody is building
  Why it matters: an 8,000 m2 pad is 0.25% of a 320 ha parcel (invisible) but
  a large share of a footprint (obvious). The AOI choice dominates every number.
  A plan filed in March 2025 may score June 2025 but never June 2024: no
  information from after the fact is allowed in.

Step 2  Fetch the imagery                                     (basc_fetch.py)
  Per site, per period, one 10 m/px Sentinel-2 L2A raster from CDSE with six
  bands (B02 B03 B04 B08 B11 B12) plus a cloud/valid mask. Optionally fetched
  with an outer buffer so the context ring is in the raster.
  Output: results/<run>/<site>/<period>.tif and a grid.json.

Step 3  Unmix each pixel into four ground types              (basc_lsma.py)
  Linear Spectral Mixture Analysis after Wu & Murray (2003). Every pixel is
  expressed as a fraction of four endmembers: vegetation, soil, high albedo
  (concrete, sand, also cloud), low albedo (water, shadow, asphalt).
  Fractions are non-negative and sum to one. Endmembers are extracted from the
  imagery itself (N-FINDR on the leading principal components), on ONE shared
  basis for all sites, so positives and negatives are read the same way.
  Thin cirrus is masked with s2cloudless because it unmixes as high albedo.

Step 4  Ask "is this pixel unusual FOR ITSELF, THIS TIME OF YEAR"  (change.py)
  Changed does not mean "soil above 0.5". It means the soil fraction is far
  above what THIS pixel usually shows in THIS calendar fortnight.
  For each pixel and fraction:
    take the same fortnight (+/-1) in the 1-3 earlier years,
    take the MEDIAN and a MAD-scaled sigma,
    z = (value - median) / sigma.
  A pixel is changed when z > k_sigma (default 2.5, swept up to 16) AND the
  absolute move is at least 0.10. A sigma floor stops tarmac and water from
  producing huge z on trivial wobble. Median/MAD, not mean/sd, so one missed
  cloud in a baseline year does not suppress real change.
  This is why a ploughed field in February does not fire: it ploughs every
  February. (February is the single largest start month, 15.8% of starts.)

Step 5  Group changed pixels into blobs                 (change.py, tiling.py)
  Changed pixels are joined with 8-connectivity into components. Tiny blobs
  (under 6 px) are dropped. Per zone, per period, the features are:
    largest component area, total changed area, % of zone affected,
    number of components, vegetation-loss area, new-soil area,
    rectangularity of the largest component.
  The minimum mapping unit is 8,000 m2: a zone counts as changed in a period
  only if enough connected area crossed that bar.

Step 6  Turn per-period flags into a call, causally             (detect.py)
  Walk the periods forward. At each one use ONLY observations at or before it.
    insufficient_evidence  fewer than 3 clear looks so far, or cloudy now
    no_change              clear looks in hand, nothing firing
    provisional_start      changed now, seen once. Actionable for the queue.
    confirmed_start        changed on 2 consecutive clear looks (one miss
                           allowed). Onset is dated to the FIRST sighting.
  Cloud is not counter-evidence: a cloudy period holds the run, it does not
  reset it. A snowed-in site is not a quiet site.

Step 7  Optional stage call, 0-15%                                (stage.py)
    0% none, 5% clearing, 10% grading, 15% foundations, "10-15" when the
    sensor cannot separate a graded pad from poured concrete.
  Foundations are never called from brightness alone (high albedo behaves
  partly as a cloud detector). Thresholds are unvalidated starting values.

Step 8  The ring gate (new, uncommitted today)          (score_features.py)
  A per-pixel seasonal baseline cancels what a pixel usually does; it cannot
  cancel what THIS YEAR does to every pixel (drought, late snowmelt, a
  ploughing season shifted a fortnight). Those fire the parcel and the ring
  together. The gate keeps a period only if the site's affected fraction
  exceeds the ring's by a swept ratio, i.e. the change is CONCENTRATED on the
  site. The gated series then goes through the same step-6 rules.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. HOW IT IS SCORED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
Test set: 36 campuses. 16 positives with a verified start and a pre-start
site plan; 20 negatives (announced or land-banked, never built), 16 of them
matched 1:1 to a positive on parcel area and season.

  negatives  nothing was ever built, so ANY confirmed call is a false alarm.
  positives  a confirmation counts only inside [start-2, start+k] periods;
             earlier than that is a false alarm, not an early detection.

Thresholds (k_sigma, tau, ring ratio) are SWEPT and reported as a curve.
Choosing one on these 36 campuses and quoting its score would be fitting on
the test set. Sweep intermediates are gitignored for that reason.

4. WHERE THE NUMBERS STAND
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Given the start date, does a start look different?   AUC 0.863 (NDVI),
                                                        CI 0.71-0.97
  Scanning every fortnight for the date (causal):       campus lift 1.5-1.7x,
                                                        only 31% of starts
                                                        confirmed within 6
                                                        periods, median lag +5
  Footprint zone instead of whole parcel, k_sigma 16:   11/16 positives,
                                                        1/20 negatives,
                                                        lift 2.06 (ceiling
                                                        2.25 at this base rate)
  Control ring gate:                                    negatives done today,
                                                        positives 5/16 done
                                                        when the machine
                                                        rebooted; not scored

Read these carefully: the AUC is the transferable number. Lift is measured at
a balanced 44% base rate and will shrink on the 932-campus pipeline. The 2.06
was swept on the same 36 campuses it is scored on and is optimistic.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. WHAT IT IS NOT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
  - Not a trained ML model. There is no fitted classifier; every threshold is
    hand-set and swept. The .pkl files one directory up are a different thing
    (a power-capacity regressor), unrelated to this detector.
  - Not a when-detector yet. It separates starters from non-starters better
    than chance, but usually finds the start late or not at all.
  - Not trained on the Supabase pct_construction_status labels. Those track
    progress AFTER a human decided construction began, so they cannot teach
    onset. Tracker ENTRY (about 10 near-onset campuses a month) is a
    validation stream, not a training set.
  - Not the production detector. Production is the Supabase function
    detect-construction-start (v27) in the ACD repo; this is the candidate
    replacement under evaluation.

6. FILE MAP
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
  basc_fetch.py          get rasters from CDSE            (step 2)
  aoi.py, inject_zones   where to look, three zones       (step 1)
  basc_lsma.py           endmembers + unmixing            (step 3)
  change.py              seasonal baseline, components    (steps 4-5)
  basc_features.py       runs 3-7 per site, per zone
  detect.py              causal three-state rules         (step 6)
  stage.py               0-15% stage call                 (step 7)
  score_features.py      scoring, sweeps, ring gate       (step 8, §3)
  basc_discriminate.py   AUC given the date
  basc_replay_causal.py  scan for the date
  pick_negatives.py      area-matched negative class
  replay.py, truth.py    period math, verified starts
  results/               per-run outputs (rf_pos_<k>, rf_neg_<k> = today's
                         ring sweep by k_sigma)
  BUILD_LOG_*.md         day-by-day findings; newest wins
